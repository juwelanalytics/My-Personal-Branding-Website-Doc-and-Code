2. Why Standard Tracking Fails on GoHighLevel Forms (The Iframe Wall)

Because GoHighLevel forms load inside cross-origin iframes, standard GTM form triggers and browser selectors are blocked by Same-Origin Policy security rules from inspecting DOM elements or detecting form submissions inside the frame[cite: 28, 29].
