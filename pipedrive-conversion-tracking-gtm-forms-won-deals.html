1. Pipedrive Knows Who Bought, Google Ads Does Not

While B2B sales teams manage pipelines inside Pipedrive, ad platforms remain disconnected from closed-won revenue unless offline conversion loops are established[cite: 18, 19]. Bridging this gap enables algorithms to optimize for actual client value[cite: 18, 19].

3. Step-by-Step Implementation Guide

Phase 1 & 2: Attribution Fields & GTM Helper

Create custom text fields in Pipedrive[cite: 18, 19]. Deploy a GTM helper script to capture and store click IDs in cookies, exposing them via `window.pd_webform`[cite: 18, 19].

Phase 3, 4 & 5: Hidden Fields & LeadBooster Chatbot

Map Pipedrive hidden form fields to JavaScript variables to ensure reliable GCLID storage[cite: 18, 19]. Use the LeadBooster API to track chatbot conversation completions[cite: 18, 19].

Phase 6, 7 & 8: Won Deals & Validation

Create offline conversion actions in Google Ads and sync closed-won revenue automatically using Zapier, Make, n8n, or Google Sheets[cite: 18, 19].

4. Troubleshooting Common Configuration Mistakes

  • Hidden Fields Stay Empty: Ensure attribution helper tags fire on Initialization All Pages and verify variable name matching[cite: 19].
  • Chatbot Event Never Fires: Load LeadBooster earlier on pages or track live chats directly from CRM logs[cite: 19].
  • Google Ads Upload Rejections: Check that GCLIDs are case-sensitive and conversion action names match character for character[cite: 19].

5. Frequently Asked Questions

What does full Pipedrive conversion tracking include?[cite: 19] +

It includes capturing click IDs on landing pages, tracking web forms and LeadBooster chatbots via GTM, storing parameters in Pipedrive fields, and importing closed-won deal revenue back to ad platforms[cite: 18, 19].

Can a Pipedrive web form store the GCLID?[cite: 19] +

Yes. By adding hidden form fields configured to read from JavaScript variables supplied by your GTM attribution helper, GCLIDs pass seamlessly into CRM contact records[cite: 18, 19].
