AUDIT AND EXECUTION WORKFLOW

How I audit, architect, and verify first-party tracking systems.

Because every business has a different situation and goal, generic paid SaaS tools often bring rigid logic limits and common attribution gaps that go unnoticed. Without grounding tracking in core concepts, teams end up with noisy data and guesswork instead of clarity. I try, test, and verify zero-cost custom systems for B2B agencies, marketers, and service-based brands—fixing tracking and data analysis so you can make data-driven decisions and scale your business.

Execution Process

From signal diagnosis to verified production deployment.

Clear checkpoints so you always know what is broken, how it will be fixed, and how results are verified.

STEP 01 / DIAGNOSTIC AUDIT

Stack, Tag, and Attribution Leak Audit

I inspect your GTM/sGTM setup, GA4 events, CRM webhooks, embedded iFrames, and ad platform conversion logs to pinpoint where click IDs (gclid, fbclid), UTMs, or conversion values drop.

STEP 02 / SYSTEM BLUEPRINT

Custom First-Party Architecture Design

I map out a clean, cost-efficient pipeline—such as Cloudflare Workers sGTM, custom cross-frame scripts, Apps Script/CRM stage sync, or BigQuery SQL schemas—tailored to your actual funnel rather than bloated SaaS add-ons.

STEP 03 / TRY, TEST AND VERIFY

Edge-Case Testing and Signal Validation

Before going live, every event, event_id deduplication pair, SHA-256 hash, consent state, and CRM stage trigger is tested across real user flows and verified in preview/debug logs.

STEP 04 / HANDOVER AND OWNERSHIP

Documentation and Full Client Control

You keep full ownership of your domains, Cloudflare/GCP accounts, scripts, and BigQuery/Looker assets along with practical handover notes so your team can scale with confidence.
