Audit and Engineering Process

Every engagement starts by inspecting how data actually moves through your site, forms, CRMs, warehouses, and ad accounts—then building and verifying a clean setup matched to your business model rather than forcing generic templates.

Audit Schedule
How We Work Together

Four structured stages from signal diagnosis to live verification.

Step 01 • Diagnostic Audit

Trace Where Data & Attribution Break

Inspect client/server tag firing, iFrame or cross-domain redirects, click-ID capture (gclid, fbclid), CRM stage webhooks, consent states, and warehouse/BI discrepancies.

Step 02 • Architecture Blueprint

Design Around Your Business Logic

Map out a practical first-party design—evaluating zero-cost Cloudflare Workers sGTM vs. managed sGTM where appropriate, custom storage scripts, offline conversion triggers, and SQL/BI reporting needs.

Step 03 • Build & Integration

Implement Clean Pipelines & Schemas

Configure server containers, SHA-256 hashed CAPI/OCT syncs, GHL/HubSpot/CallRail workflows, Consent Mode v2 mappings, Segment/Matomo routing, or BigQuery SQL and Looker Studio views.

Step 04 • Test & Verify

Validate End-to-End Before Handover

Test real user journeys across devices, consent states, embedded frames, and CRM stage transitions so every event, parameter, and downstream report is verified in action.
