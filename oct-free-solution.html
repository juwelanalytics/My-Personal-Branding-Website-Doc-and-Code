1. The Reality of the B2B & Service-Based Customer Journey

In e-commerce, a user clicks an ad, adds a product to the cart, and pays within 5 minutes. B2B and service businesses operate on a completely different timeline where leads take days or weeks to convert into paying deals.

2. What is Offline Conversion Tracking (OCT) & Why Do You Need It?

Offline Conversion Tracking captures unique click IDs (like GCLID or fbclid) along with first-party PII at form submission, storing them in your CRM/Sheets, and pushing a server signal back to ad platforms only when a deal turns into a qualified client.
